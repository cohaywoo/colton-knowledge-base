## Farm design principles

An effective mob farm controls where mobs can spawn, how they move, and how drops are collected. Spawn-proofing nearby areas concentrates spawning in the intended space and improves the farm's output.

## Safety and efficiency

Separate the spawning, transport, and collection areas so the farm is easy to troubleshoot. Use walls, lighting, and fall protection to keep hostile mobs from escaping. The best design is not always the fastest one; reliability, accessibility, and safe item collection matter too.

## Choosing a farm

- Crop and animal farms provide dependable food and renewable materials.
- Hostile mob farms produce items such as bones, string, and gunpowder.
- Specialized farms should be built near the locations and dimensions where their mechanics are most effective.
