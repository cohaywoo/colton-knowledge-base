## Crafting fundamentals

Crafting transforms gathered materials into tools, equipment, and infrastructure. A crafting table expands the available recipes, while a furnace, smoker, and blast furnace provide efficient ways to process food and ores.

## Useful progression

Early recipes should support survival first: tools, torches, a shield, a bed, and storage. Later, prioritize an enchanting table, brewing equipment, and durable diamond or netherite gear. Planning recipes around shared materials reduces wasted resources and keeps progression efficient.

## Resource planning

- Keep wood, iron, coal, and food stocked before beginning a major project.
- Use labeled chests to separate building blocks, valuables, tools, and farming supplies.
- Craft durable equipment only after gathering enough materials for repairs or replacements.
