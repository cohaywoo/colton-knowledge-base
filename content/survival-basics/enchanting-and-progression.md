# Enchanting and Progression

Enchanting is most effective after the basic survival loop is already dependable. A player who has food, shelter, storage, and a safe mine can spend experience on durable improvements instead of repeatedly rebuilding lost starter tools. The enchanting table is only the beginning: bookshelves, lapis lazuli, and a steady source of experience make the system worth maintaining.

## Build the Foundation

An enchanting table requires diamonds, obsidian, and a book, so the resource planning in [[mining-and-ore-safety|mining and ore safety]] matters. Sugar cane from [[food-and-farming|food and farming]] becomes paper, and paper plus leather becomes books. Place bookshelves around the table with one block of air between the shelf and table; extra blocks or decorations in that gap can prevent the shelves from contributing power.

### Spend Experience Carefully

Early enchantments should improve the tools used every day. Efficiency speeds up routine mining, Unbreaking extends durability, and Protection makes ordinary exploration more forgiving. Fortune is especially valuable on the right mining tool, while Silk Touch supports a separate style of collecting blocks. Do not enchant the first item just because the offered choices are available; repair or combine equipment when the material cost is sensible.

#### A Long-Term Checklist

- Keep lapis and spare books in the same storage area as the enchanting table.
- Maintain a renewable food supply before committing to long mining or experience trips.
- Test an enchantment on a tool you can replace before using rare materials.

The quiet advantage is preparation. A well-lit shelter makes the table safe to use, and a bed from the [[first-night-plan|first-night plan]] keeps travel predictable. With these systems connected, progression feels like an expansion of the base rather than a desperate search for one lucky item.
