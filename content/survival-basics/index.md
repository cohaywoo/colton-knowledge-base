Focuses on early-game essentials, such as punching trees for wood, building a temporary dirt or wooden shelter, lighting up the area with torches, and crafting basic stone tools.

## First-day priorities

The first day should focus on securing the essentials: collect wood, craft basic tools, gather food, and create a safe shelter before nightfall. A bed, torches, and a furnace make early exploration much safer and establish a reliable starting point.

## Progression strategy

Survival progression works best when resources are invested in the right order. Upgrade from wooden to stone tools, then prioritize iron for a shield, bucket, armor, and dependable tools. Once the base is secure, diamonds and enchantments can support larger builds and dangerous expeditions.

## Practical habits

- Keep a spare food supply and tools in every long-distance expedition.
- Mark routes with torches, signs, or coordinates so important locations are easy to find.
- Store valuable materials in an organized base before exploring unfamiliar areas.
