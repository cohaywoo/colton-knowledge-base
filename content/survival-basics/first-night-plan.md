# First-Night Plan

The first Minecraft day is a short logistics problem. The goal is not to build a perfect house; it is to create enough safety, light, tools, and food that the night cannot end the world before the real projects begin. Move with purpose and collect materials in an order that prevents backtracking.

## The First Ten Minutes

Punch a few logs, turn them into planks and sticks, and craft a wooden pickaxe. Mine stone immediately, then replace the wooden tools with a stone pickaxe, axe, and sword. Collect extra cobblestone for a furnace and a small reserve of tools. While moving, gather seeds, visible animals, and at least a few pieces of food. Do not chase every resource; returning to a safe location matters more than filling the inventory.

### Before Sunset

Find coal or make charcoal from logs, craft torches, and choose a shelter location near trees and water. If a village is nearby, its buildings can provide temporary protection, but light the paths and avoid opening unexplored caves at night. A bed is the best early milestone because sleeping skips the most dangerous part of the first cycle.

#### A Reliable Checklist

- Make a stone pickaxe, axe, sword, furnace, and torches.
- Secure food and place a bed before exploring after dark.
- Mark the route home with torches or distinctive blocks.

The temporary shelter described in [[shelter-and-lighting|shelter and lighting]] can be ugly and small. That is fine. Its job is to buy time for the renewable food plan in [[food-and-farming|food and farming]], not to become a permanent monument on the first evening.
